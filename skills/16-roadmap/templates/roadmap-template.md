# MVP Roadmap

## MVP definition

One short paragraph from `scope.md`: what must work for this to count as a stable MVP, and what is explicitly out.

## Stages

Stage statuses: `planned` | `active` | `partial` | `parked` | `done` | `moved` | `dropped`. `parked` means intentionally deferred but still in the MVP; `moved` means transferred elsewhere; `dropped` means removed from the MVP. Only the developer changes a stage to `done`, `moved`, or `dropped`; `active` also requires explicit activation permission.
